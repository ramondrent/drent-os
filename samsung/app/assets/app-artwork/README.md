# Personal launcher app cards

Locally bundled 16:9 cards composed from service marks; no remote images or promotional content are loaded. Backgrounds and layout are custom. Brand names and marks remain property of their owners; these assets do not imply endorsement or grant trademark rights.

- Plex: Simple Icons v15, https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@15/icons/plex.svg (CC0; licence in ../icons/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt). Recoloured gold.
- YouTube: existing bundled Simple Icons mark; see ../icons/SOURCES.json and licence. White on red.
- NPO Start: https://npo.nl/start/favicon.svg, retrieved 2026-10-23. Official colour mark, placed on charcoal. Copyright remains with NPO; no open licence asserted.
- KIJK: inline home logo from https://www.kijk.nl/, retrieved 2026-10-23. White mark on a custom purple background. Copyright remains with Talpa; no open licence asserted.

NPO and KIJK assets are for this personal launcher experiment. Review rights before any public redistribution.

Ramon OS uses transparent monochrome adaptations of these same marks on the shared glass tiles. The NPO diamond is white with dark internal lettering for contrast. No logo sources were changed.
